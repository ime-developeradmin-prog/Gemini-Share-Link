## Strategic Recommendations

Based on the hardware environment and the current parameters visible, here are the primary adjustments to consider for stability and performance:

### 1\. IOMMU Parameter Refinement

The bare `iommu` flag is sometimes ambiguous depending on the kernel version. For an Intel-based system, it is often more effective to be explicit:

- **Recommended change:** Replace `iommu` with `intel_iommu=on` and `iommu=pt`.

- **Rationale:**`iommu=pt` (passthrough) prevents the kernel from attempting to manage DMA for devices that don't need it, which can resolve timing issues with NVIDIA drivers and improve disk I/O performance.


### 2\. Graphics Initialization (NVIDIA/Intel Hybrid)

Since you are configuring a hybrid environment, the `rhgb quiet` flags (Red Hat Graphical Boot) hide the early kernel log. While aesthetically pleasing, they mask errors during driver initialization.

- **Debugging Recommendation:** Temporarily remove `rhgb quiet` if you encounter hangs or "black screen" issues during the transition from GRUB to the login manager.

- **NVIDIA-specific flag:** If you encounter screen tearing or synchronization issues with the NVIDIA card, adding `nvidia-drm.modeset=1` is standard practice for modern Linux kernels to ensure the Direct Rendering Manager is properly utilized.


### 3\. Power Management & ACPI
